3. How the WhatsApp agent and leads agent work

Two services, each with one job: YCloud/Meta carries the messages and Groq writes the reply. Everything else runs inside the O3 app on your server: the customer's car, the price list, bookings, the inbox, the handover rules.

1. Customer writes"Price for foam wash?" on WhatsApp, or fills an Instagram/Facebook ad form
→
2. Lead createdThe O3 app saves the lead and finds the customer and car if known
→
3. Smart replyThe app sends Groq the studio's own services, prices for that car, packages, timings and FAQs, and gets a short answer
→
4. Reply sentThe answer goes out on the studio number within seconds
→
5. Staff see allEvery chat is in Admin → WhatsApp inbox; staff can take over

Are the replies smart?

Yes. Answers come from the studio's own information, not general internet knowledge. For example: "Foam wash for your Innova is ₹599. We have 10 am or 4 pm tomorrow, shall I book one?" It replies in the customer's language: English, Hindi, Gujarati or Hinglish. You improve it by adding FAQs in Admin → Leads → Knowledge base; no retraining is needed.

Safety built in

  • Hands over to staff on complaints, anger, refunds or "talk to a person", and tells the customer someone will reply.
  • Never invents prices: it only quotes what is in the app.
  • Never goes silent: if Groq is down or the daily free limit is used up, the studio's own agent replies from live services, prices, slots and FAQs, or hands over to staff.
  • Staff stay in control: staff can keep chatting from the WhatsApp Business app on the phone. Their messages are free and don't interfere with the agent.

Cost control

  • AI: ₹0. Groq's free tier has no card on file, so there is nothing to overspend.
  • Customer replies: the first 1,000 a month are free from Meta, then ₹0.115 each.
  • Staff messages from the phone app: free.

4. Step 1: connect the services (day 1)

Never send keys or passwords in chat. They go straight into the server settings during step 2.

4.1 WhatsApp through YCloud, keeping the same number you + client me

The client needs: the studio phone in hand, GST or Udyam certificate, owner's PAN, a debit/credit card, and a Facebook login for the business owner.

  1. If the number is on the normal WhatsApp app, move it to the free WhatsApp Business app on the same phone. WhatsApp offers to keep the chats during the switch. Update the app to the latest version.
  2. Sign up at ycloud.com with the studio email → Free plan.
  3. In YCloud: WhatsApp → Connect → "Connect WhatsApp Business App" (coexistence). Log in with the owner's Facebook and create or select the business portfolio. Pick the studio number, then scan the QR code / confirm on the phone and choose Share chats, so up to 6 months of history syncs.
  4. In Meta Business settings: start business verification with the GST or Udyam certificate. Messaging works before verification is finished, with a starter limit of new customers per day.
  5. Top up the YCloud wallet: ₹500 is plenty to start. This pays Meta's message charges at Meta's own rate.
  6. Create two templates in YCloud → Templates. Approval takes minutes to a day:
    • o3_service_update: Utility, English, body "Hi {{1}}, {{2}}". This sends booking confirmations, reminders, bills and alerts.
    • o3_login_code: Authentication, English, with a Copy-code button. This sends login codes.
  7. In YCloud → Developers: create an API key. Webhook URL: https://YOUR-DOMAIN/api/whatsapp/webhook, events inbound message and message updated. Add the key during step 2.

Me: add the YCloud connection to the app (it currently connects to Meta directly or AiSensy) and switch login codes to WhatsApp.

4.2 Groq for the AI agent you · 5 min

  1. console.groq.com → sign up with the studio email. No card needed.
  2. API Keys → Create API key "o3-server" → keep it for step 2. It is shown only once.
  3. Don't add billing. On the free plan the agent can never cost money.

4.3 Instagram / Facebook ad leads through Make you me · 20 min

  1. Sign up at make.com (free plan: 1,000 operations a month, roughly 500 leads).
  2. New scenario: Facebook Lead Ads → "Watch new leads". Log in with the Facebook account that runs the studio's ads, and pick the Page and form.
  3. Add HTTP → Make a request: POST to https://YOUR-DOMAIN/api/leads/capture, with the header and fields I give you (the secret lead key comes from Admin → Leads → Connect lead sources).
  4. Turn the scenario on and submit a test lead from Meta's Lead Ads testing tool.
Cheaper still: "Click-to-WhatsApp" adsAds with a "Send WhatsApp message" button bring customers straight to the AI agent. No form or connector is needed, and Meta makes messages in that chat free for a set window after the customer taps the ad.

4.4 Gmail app password for app emails you · 5 min

Studio Gmail → Google Account → Security → turn on 2-Step Verification → App passwords → create "O3 app" → keep the 16-letter password for step 2.

4.5 Google Play developer account you · 20 min

Start it on day 1 because Google verifies identity. Which account to use is decided in section 6.

  2. me Upload the new version and run the database updates. They only add new tables and columns.

6. Step 3: wrap for Android with Capacitor (day 2) me

  • Capacitor is free. It turns the web app into a real Android app (.aab file) with:
    • the O3 icon and splash screen
    • camera access for check-in and job photos
    • location only while the app is open, which avoids Google's extra background-location review
  • The app loads its screens from your server. So after launch, most fixes and design changes go live with a server update and no new Play review. Only icon, permission or native changes need a Play update.
  • Google Play also requires:
    • a public privacy policy page
    • an account-deletion option, inside the app and as a web page
    I add both, plus terms and refund pages.
  • I create the signing key. You must store it safely for ever: every future update needs it.
  • I prepare the listing:
    • name, short and full description
    • icon 512×512
    • feature graphic 1024×500
    • phone screenshots
    • the answers for Google's data-safety form

7. Step 4: Google Play (day 3)

Which developer account? This decides how fast the app is public

OptionPublic on PlayNotes
A. An account you already own: your own or your agency's Play account, if it is a business account or a personal one created before 13 Nov 2023Days 3–10, after Google's review (usually under 7 days)Fastest, and allowed. Developers often publish client apps from their own account. Later the app can be moved to the client's account with Google's official app transfer; customers keep it and its reviews.
B. New personal account (client's or yours), made todayAbout day 17–24Google requires 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row, then a production review of up to 7 days. On day 3 the app is submitted and approved for closed testing, and the studio's staff and regular customers can install it from Play straight away as testers.
C. New business account, client's ownWeeksNeeds a D-U-N-S number (can take up to ~30 days) and Google's business verification. No tester rule. Best long-term home; start in parallel and transfer the app later.
Don't buy someone else's Play account
  • It breaks Google's rules: developer accounts can't be sold or transferred.
  • Google links accounts: it ties accounts together by identity, payment card and device.
  • The risk: a bought account can be closed without warning, taking the app, its reviews and every linked account with it, including the client's future one.
Paid "12 testers" services carry a similar risk. Use real people: studio staff, family and regular customers.

Recommended: use option A if you have an eligible account. Otherwise do B today, so the 14-day clock starts on day 3. Meanwhile customers use the live website, which installs like an app.

Publishing steps in Play Console you, with my texts and images

  1. Create app: name "O3 Car Spaa", English (India), App, Free.
  2. Fill in each form under "Set up your app":
    • privacy policy link
    • app access: the reviewer login I provide
    • ads: none
    • content rating questionnaire
    • target audience 18+
    • news, government and health app: no
    • data safety: the answers I provide
    • account-deletion link
  3. Store listing: texts, icon, feature graphic, screenshots, category Auto & Vehicles, contact email.
  4. Testing → Internal testing → upload the .aab (accept Play App Signing) → install on your phones → quick check.
  5. Then, by option:
    • Option A: Production → India → same .aab → send for review.
    • Option B: Closed testing → add at least 12 testers' Gmail addresses → send for review. Keep all 12 opted in for 14 days, then click "Apply for production".
